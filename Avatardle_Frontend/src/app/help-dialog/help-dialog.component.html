Guess who said the quote. Look for familiar turns of phrase and clues to the moment.

Three ways to find your answer

Three hints unlock as you make more guesses.

  1. 01

    Next line

    The line spoken immediately after the quote.

  2. 02

    Previous line

    The line spoken immediately before the quote.

  3. 03

    Episode name

    The episode in which the quote is spoken.

The first or last line?

If the quote opens or closes an episode, the hints will tell you.
